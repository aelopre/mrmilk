WR vs CB, Week 8

Each receiver's likely corner, from where he lines up (left, right or slot) and the corner the defense lists there. It's not shadow coverage, so treat it as a tiebreaker. Green = favorable, red = tough. CB rank is out of 130 corners (1 = best). Proj effect = how much the coverage matchup and scheme move his receiving-yards projection (net). His total snaps = the receiver's projected snaps for the whole game (his share of plays over his last 3 games × his team's pace), not snaps against that corner. CB % of D tgts = how many of the defense's targets that corner was the man in coverage on, a reminder that one corner rarely covers anyone all game. CB EPA/tgt in scheme = the corner's 2026 EPA allowed per target in man and in zone, weighted by how often his defense plays each; higher = easier for the receiver. Pick a team above to filter.

Run defense by lane

Designed runs only (no scrambles, kneels or QB sneaks). Inside = up the middle or between the guards, off tackle = the tackle gaps, outside = around the end. Success = the run gained enough to keep the offense on schedule. Explosive = 10+ yards, stuffed = no gain or a loss. Light box = 6 or fewer defenders in the box. Short yardage = 3rd or 4th and 2 or less. Green = soft (good for the runner), red = stingy. Rank 1 = softest run defense by EPA per run. Click a header to sort.
